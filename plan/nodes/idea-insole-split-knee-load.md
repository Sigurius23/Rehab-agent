# Insole left/right load split against force plates, and its effect on knee load

A testable hypothesis, not an established reduction in uncertainty: bilateral insole estimates against force plates in slow squats, and how they change the knee-load outputs. The ±18 % figure came from one sensitivity analysis, not a validated error bound. The force-fit, saturation and reportability gates stay; insoles do not give the full 3D ground force.
