# A personalised multi-segment foot, fitted separately for each foot

A standard multi-segment foot model, fitted separately for each foot, with parameters such as: foot length,
width and external shape; arch shape and height under a documented loading condition; heel, midfoot, forefoot and
toe proportions; ankle and foot movement, including the arch during stance where capture supports it; strength,
mobility and contact parameters where measured or defensibly estimated. Each parameter goes in the registry with
its source, uncertainty and permitted uses ([[idea-parameter-registry]]).

The model should show the person's foot and its approximate movement. It must not imply that the individual
tarsal bones, ligaments or plantar fascia were measured. Candidates: the foot-model comparison
([[sole-foot-model-comparison]]).
