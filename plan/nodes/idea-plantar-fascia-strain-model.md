# The 2016 OpenSim plantar-fascia strain model as a comparator

The first slip of the plantar fascia as a path with MTP wrapping, combined with a geometric arch model, used for running strain estimates in 18 runners (PLOS). A small, focused comparator for the fascia in the D'Hondt port.
