# Peripheral nerve layer: nerve fibre and bundle models

PyFibers and NRV model individual nerve fibres and bundles electrically (PyFibers reproduces 11 published fibre models). They are mainly electrical-stimulation research tools: neither gives a pressure-to-firing model for a person's foot. A later, specialised layer. Open3Dmodel can supply labelled nerve geometry; its assets have individual and share-alike terms to review.
