# The simulated foot-to-ground model, checked against measurement

The virtual sole in OpenSim, checked in three separate stages (`VALIDATION_PLAN.md`): A, force plates; B,
regional barefoot pressure; C, foot–insole contact. Agreement between simulations is not validation.
