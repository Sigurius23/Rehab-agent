# Twin layers from everyday measurements (Tier C and B)
What the person model builds today from everyday data (`docs/person-model.md`): bones that stay the same across
sessions, the body's surface and mass, muscle capacity, and the person's own alignment. Built and tested on
synthetic sessions and one real squat; the bones from video are Tier C until checked against imaging.
