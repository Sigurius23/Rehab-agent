# Personalise for movement, not only shape (the foot-ground interface as a model)

A foot scan says what shape a foot is, not how the person moves on it. Add a time dimension to the foot specification, make the foot-ground interface a first-class model, and later explore passive-haptic or exoskeleton interfaces, in a loop kept separate from the core twin: capture, personalised foot and motion model, optional pressure or force data, proposed interface, physical test, update. Deforming a rigged surface is not a measurement of tissue strain or force.
