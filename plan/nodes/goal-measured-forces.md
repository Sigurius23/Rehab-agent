# Ground forces and plantar pressure measured, in the lab and in daily life

The simulated loads (muscles, joints, assistance) currently rest on estimated ground forces (`--grf-sensitivity`
bands). Measured forces come from force plates in the lab and, once validated, from the sensor sole in daily life.

Related: the force-plate checks of the simulated foot-ground contact, [[sole-forceplate-quasistatic]] and
[[sole-forceplate-dynamic]].
