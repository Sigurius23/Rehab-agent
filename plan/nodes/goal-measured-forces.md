# Muscle and exoskeleton numbers rest on measured ground forces

Muscle demand, joint loads and exoskeleton sizing currently rest on estimated ground forces (`--grf-sensitivity`
bands). They need measured forces: force plates, or the sensor sole once it is validated.

Related: the force-plate checks of the simulated sole, [[sole-forceplate-quasistatic]] and
[[sole-forceplate-dynamic]].
