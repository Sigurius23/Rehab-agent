# Personalised footwear from the foot scan: last, sole and five-toe shoe

The owner's capability map compares 20 shoe and last design tools on 26 features (520 rated cells, 20 flagged
as unconfirmed rather than guessed), along six stages: capture the foot, clean the mesh, create the last, design the
upper and pattern, design the sole and footbed, prepare for printing.
- **No single tool spans all six stages**; every realistic project stitches at least three together.
- **Pick the route first, the tool second.** Five routes lead from a foot to a last, for example direct scan to
  last (fastest to a truly custom shape, but a bad scan becomes a bad last without warning) or a base last morphed
  to the foot.
- **Strongest overall:** Materialise (3-matic, Phits, Footscan), Shoemaster, Rhino with Grasshopper. **Open tools:**
  FreeCAD, OpenSCAD, Blender, MeshLab, CloudCompare and Open3D, Open Shoe Designer.

It joins the personalised foot ([[twin-personalised-foot]]), the sensor sole that must fit the shoe
([[goal-sensor-sole]]) and personalising for movement ([[idea-movement-personalisation]]). Whether the map's own
files go into the repository: [[d-shoe-cad-map-files]].
