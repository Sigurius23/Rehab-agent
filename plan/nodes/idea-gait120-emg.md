# Validate muscle activations against EMG (Gait120)

Held-out motion, force and EMG data for the muscle estimates. Gait120 has 120 participants with right-leg EMG, but they are all healthy adult men, so it cannot on its own show that the estimates hold for everyone.

## Resources

- [Gait120 (Scientific Data)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-025-05391-0)

More held-out datasets from the owner's research note on fascia, nerve and motor-control models, 2026-11-04:
- [10-person high-density EMG](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-023-02679-x): below-knee muscles of the dominant leg, with motion capture, force plates and IMUs;
- [Camargo / EPIC](https://www.epic.gatech.edu/opensource-biomechanics-camargo-et-al/): 22 participants, motion capture, force plates, IMU and EMG on level ground, stairs and ramps.
Check each dataset's file terms.
