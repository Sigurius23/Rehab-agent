# Joint angles measured against a reference, per joint and movement

Joint angles and posture measures with a measured error against a reference, per joint, plane and movement.

Where it stands (`docs/roadmap.md`, `docs/validation-results.md`): the back-end is verified against synthetic
truth; agreement with OpenCap is measured (5 cameras, within 2.5–6° on trunk, pelvis and FPPA); there is no
accuracy figure against marker motion capture yet.
