# Measured pressure response of the film

The expected result above is a proposal (2026-11-09). Confirm or change its numbers before the first coupon is
pressed.

- **Full scale** is the highest pressure the coupon is loaded to. No document sets it yet. It should cover the
  plantar pressures of the movements the sole is for, so it is set, from published plantar-pressure data, before
  the first press.
- **Prediction, written before the test:** the Velostat-class film misses on hysteresis or creep, as its
  reputation suggests (`DESIGN.md` section 4). The coated textile is more likely to qualify.
- **If no material qualifies,** the result says which criterion each one misses. A per-session calibration may
  then compensate. That is a design decision for the owner, not a pass.
