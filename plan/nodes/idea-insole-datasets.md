# Open insole datasets for checking the sole's readings

For the later validation stages (barefoot regional pressure, foot-insole contact; `hardware/sensor-sole/VALIDATION_PLAN.md`). Insole-GAITRite is the best external reference for gait events and synchronisation. Check each dataset's terms before use; some have no licence file, and the Smart-Insole Dataset is non-commercial, on request.

## Resources

- [Insole-GAITRite (CC BY 4.0)](https://zenodo.org/records/19662017)
- [MovePort, 230 sensors per foot with EMG (CC BY 4.0)](https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/MovePort_Multimodal_Dataset_of_EMG_IMU_MoCap_and_Insole_Pressure_for_Analyzing_Abnormal_Movements_and_Postures_in_Rehabilitation_Training/25202183)
- [Mobilise-D TVS (CC BY 4.0)](https://zenodo.org/records/13899386)
- [48-FSR plantar pressure insoles (CC BY 4.0)](https://zenodo.org/records/21743536)
- [GRFMS, with a Vicon reference (CC BY 4.0)](https://doi.org/10.17632/xgw6bg3g8h.1)
- [TG0, force-plate 3D ground forces (no licence file)](https://github.com/tg0uk/Smart_Insole_Database)
