# The knee's projection angle only where it is well conditioned

In the pilot's two-legged squat, the motion capture's own knee frontal-plane projection angle ranges over 59° on the left and 22° on the right. Against it the best route is 22.9° off on the right and 5.9° on the left; against OpenCap the sides were the other way round (5.2° and 9.6°).

A likely cause, not yet checked: the angle is a projection, and it becomes ill-conditioned as the thigh nears horizontal in a deep squat. A first diagnostic is the error and the reference's value against knee flexion. If that holds, the knee-valgus cue would use the angle only up to a flexion limit, or another measure of the knee's sideways position.

The cue rules use this angle, so it matters before any knee cue is reviewed ([[cues-clinician-review]]).
