# Phone-only foot capture: protocol, consent plan and a repeatability pilot

From the research note's next steps:
1. A short capture protocol (lighting, a measured scale target, still foot and toes, high-overlap views) and a
   participant consent and retention plan ([[D-07]]).
2. A candidate reconstruction route as an experiment: device compatibility, compute cost, licences, local or cloud
   handling.
3. A surface coverage and repeatability pilot on a few volunteers, left and right, with the load state recorded,
   and robust rejection and missing-region reporting. No accuracy claims.
Photo against depth-assisted capture is compared in [[idea-foot-capture-comparison]].
