# Model assumptions to correct: patellar-tendon ratio and co-contraction

The patellar-tendon to quadriceps force ratio is 1.0 in the model against about 0.7 in cadavers (printed as a caveat, not corrected). Static optimisation systematically under-counts co-contraction of opposing muscles.
